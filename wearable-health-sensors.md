Wearable health sensors are electronic devices designed to collect physiological information from the human body.
Examples include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and medical monitoring devices.
Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) in Wearable Devices
Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) is a wireless communication technology designed for devices that need to exchange small amounts of data while consuming relatively little power.

In wearable healthcare technology, BLE allows devices such as smartwatches and heart-rate monitors to communicate with smartphones.
